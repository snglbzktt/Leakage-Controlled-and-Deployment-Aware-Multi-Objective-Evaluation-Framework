Table 5. Locked omnibus and verified post-hoc statistical analysis across the compression/deployment endpoints. Each omnibus stratum contains five paired seed blocks and eleven variants. Monte Carlo Friedman tests are reported with Kendall's W effect size. Ten exact paired post-hoc comparisons against B0 were evaluated per stratum and adjusted with Holm correction. Although all 16 omnibus tests rejected at the nominal level, none of the 160 post-hoc comparisons survived Holm correction. With five paired seeds, the minimum attainable two-sided exact p-value was 0.0625; failure to reject is therefore not interpreted as equivalence.
