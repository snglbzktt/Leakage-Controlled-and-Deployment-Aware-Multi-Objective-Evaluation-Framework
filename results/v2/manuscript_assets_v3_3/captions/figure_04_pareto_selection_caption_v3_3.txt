Figure 4. Multi-objective candidate selection under the locked balanced profile. The plot shows 18 compressed candidates in the accuracy–model-size plane. Eleven candidates passed the balanced eligibility gates and five were Pareto non-dominated. TinyML-MLP P50-QAT was selected under the balanced and relaxed profiles, while TinyML-MLP P50-FP32-FT was selected under the strict sensitivity profile. Selection followed the predeclared eligibility–Pareto–deployment ordering rule; no weighted score and no pairwise p-value gate were used.
