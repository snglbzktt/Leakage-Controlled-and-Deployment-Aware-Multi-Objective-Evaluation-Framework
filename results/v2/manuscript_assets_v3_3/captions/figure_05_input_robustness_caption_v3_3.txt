Figure 5. Input robustness across nine locked perturbation conditions for TinyML-MLP B0, the selected TinyML-MLP P50-QAT model family, and HGB B0. TinyML-MLP B0 and P50-QAT retained substantially higher Macro-F1 under the tested perturbations, whereas HGB showed pronounced sensitivity to Gaussian noise and scale drift. Five severe-fragility evaluations were observed, all for HGB. This analysis did not retrain models, did not use test results for model selection, and did not alter the locked final model.
